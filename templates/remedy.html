Stay Hydrated

Simple health information and basic self-care guidance.

🌿 What you can do

💧 Drink Water Drink clean water regularly throughout the day.
🥤 Stay Hydrated Increase fluids during hot weather or after sweating.
🍉 Eat Water-Rich Foods Fruits and vegetables can also contribute to hydration.
🛌 Take Rest Give your body enough time to recover when you are unwell.

⚠️ When to get medical help

If you cannot keep fluids down, feel extremely weak, become confused, faint, or have signs of serious dehydration, seek medical attention promptly.
